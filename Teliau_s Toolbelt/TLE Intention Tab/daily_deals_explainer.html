What is the Daily Deals tab?

The Daily Deals tab is a configuration sheet that defines the 6 daily offers shown to the player every day. Each row is one candidate offer. Together they form the loot pool the game draws from to fill the 6 slots, with weighted randomness and a budget check per slot.

The 5 column groups

High Level Items Logic

Defines each item that can be offered: discount, gem price, and which slots it's allowed to appear in.

High Level Slot Logic

Defines each of the 6 slots: gem-price min/max budget, target % of pet upgrades, and shard auto-weighting.

Offer Specifics

One row per concrete offer in a slot's loot pool: quantity, currency type, weight, and the price the player pays.

Helpers

Computed columns that validate each offer (gem-equivalent value, range check ✅/❌, % share inside the slot).

Lootpool Options Generator

The final output consumed by the game: pool id, item, weight, probability %, and unlock requirement.

From config to player

1. ItemsEach item has a base price + which slots it's allowed in.
2. SlotsEach of the 6 slots defines a price range & pet ratio.
3. OffersItem × quantity × currency = a concrete offer with a weight.
4. HelpersFormulas validate the offer fits the slot's budget.
5. PoolGame draws from the slot pool using the final probabilities.
Mental model: the tab is a weighted lottery per slot. The slot defines the rules of the lottery; the rows define the tickets and how many copies of each ticket are in the box.

The 6 slots at a glance

Slots get progressively more expensive. Lower slots favour cheap utility items; higher slots are dominated by pet upgrades (dynamic_owned_pet) which become the main sink of value once the player has unlocked them.

How probability is computed

probability % = weight ÷ Σ(weights of all eligible offers in the slot)
Eligible = passes the reach_world_X requirement and has ~Price in Range = TRUE.

FAQ

Why are some rows empty in the left columns?

The left block (High Level Items / Slot Logic) only needs one row per item / per slot. The right block (Offer Specifics + Generator) repeats one row per concrete offer, so it is much taller — that's why the left columns look empty further down.

What is the Shard Weight Calculator?

An optional auto-weight helper for shard offers (dynamic_owned_pet). When activated it distributes weights using a normal-like curve around a Center (0 = lowest, 1 = highest) with a configurable Deviation (0 = all on the center value, 1000 = uniform). When off, the manual ~Weight column wins.

What does REWARDED vs CURRENCY mean?

REWARDED = the player gets the item for free after watching a rewarded ad (ad_rewarded). CURRENCY = the player pays with an in-game currency, usually currency.gems or currency.coins.

How do I add a new offer?

Add a row in the Offer Specifics block: pick the slot id (~poolId), the item (~Selected Item), the quantity, the price, and a weight. The helpers will tell you immediately if the price is out of the slot's range.

Why do high slots show so many dynamic_owned_pet rows?

Pet upgrades scale linearly with shard count, so each shard quantity is its own row. To cover the wide gem range of slots 5 & 6 the sheet enumerates many shard amounts (10, 15, 20… 150) so the helper can place each at the right probability.
