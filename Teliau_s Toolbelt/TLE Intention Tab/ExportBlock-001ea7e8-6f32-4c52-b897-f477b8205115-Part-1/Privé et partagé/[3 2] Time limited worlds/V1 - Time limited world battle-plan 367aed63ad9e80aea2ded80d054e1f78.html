    • Rebloom mechanic:
      • I understood that the intent behind the "rebloom" mechanic is to limit how long players engage with the feature in a single session.
      • The reboot happens every X amount of time (the example given is 2 hours).
        • I interpret that as: "We want the player to come back multiple times rather than play one long session."
      1. Maybe what we can do is have different difficulty levels within the biome (small, medium, large), with the idea that less engaged players will only clear the small resources while the most engaged ones will take the time to do the big ones.
      1. We can also have the approach of “players are going to clear one biome entierly and maybe come back for the rebloom”
    • Expected engagement per player engagement type
      Engagement behaviorBase daily playtimeExpected daily time in eventRessources clearance Buy the “rebloom” ?Example of expected progression
      Light305SmallNoFirst reward of the gauge or first world rewards if multiple worlds
      Regular6020Small - MediumNoGood medium of the gauge or few worlds rewards if multiple worlds
      Invested10060Small - Medium - BigSometimesFinal gauge reward or final world reward if multiple worlds
      Elite180120Small - Medium - BigYesFinal leaderboard reward
    • Rebloom Limitation
      • Light players will clear half the map and not come back.
      • Regular players will clear the map, and occasionally come back to reclear it, but won't use rebloom (opportunistic gameplay).
      • Invested players will clear the map and be serious about refreshes ("I know I need to come back in a few hours"). They'll also be willing to spend a few reblooms to catch up from time to time (I missed a cycle, but I still want the final reward).
      • Elite players will clear everything, often, and will pay whatever it takes to get the leaderboard rewards (within the limits set out in the table above).
    • Ressource Clearance
      • Light players will only clear the Small ones,
      • Regular players will clear the Small and Medium ones,
      • Invested players will clear the Small, Medium, and Big ones,
      • Elite players will clear the Small, Medium, and Big ones, and spam the Reblooms.
    • Based on those two ways to approaching the problem, we can thing about few first great ideas for this problem:
      1. Ideally, clearing the map should grant one to two tiers of reward (never less than one, and probably the only times it's just one are in the first events). The portion of that tier should be reachable through small resources.
      1. So I can set it up so that clearing half the map once brings in enough event_xp to unlock the first threshold (first reward), and the portion that clears half the map could be small + medium resources.
      1. Then, I can set it up so that every time you come back to the event after a rebloom and clear the entire map, you gain one threshold.
      1. Then, I can set it up so that the further you progress in event levels, the more the number of rewards increases. Up to a limit for UX reasons, and from that point on, the difficulty of clearing the map starts to increase.
      1. Then, I can set things up to create bots to simulate a leaderboard.
        • The leaderboard will need to be set up in such a way that the bots challenge the player so that they become first on the leaderboard once the full number of hours planned for an event has elapsed (in this case 2h, equivalent to logging in 12 times).
